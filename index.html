- - THE QUEST - -

Every man is living
inside a story.

But not every man has recognized the part he has been called to play.

FORGED 2026: The Quest is a one-day men's conference about accepting the call God has placed before you. Calling, courage, the stories that shape us, the enemies we still face, and the kind of life that becomes a legend worth passing down.

You do not need someone else's gifts or someone else's story.
The quest has found you.

- - FOUR LEVELS - -

The Quest Log

LVL 1
QUEST I

The Quest Has Found You

Why me?

Gideon is hiding, afraid, and painfully aware of what he lacks. God addresses him according to divine calling rather than Gideon's self-assessment. You do not have to become another man before you can obey God.

CALLING
LVL 2
QUEST II

The Stories We Tell Are the Stories We Live

What kind of man am I becoming?

Stories train desire. They give us heroes, villains, goals, fears, and visions of the good life. Become intentional about what you read, watch, admire, and place before your children.

FORMATION
LVL 3
QUEST III

A Dead Dragon and Snakes in the Grass

If Christ has won, why is there still a battle?

The dragon has been slain, but the world still bears the effects of its rebellion. We do not fight for Christ's victory. We learn to live faithfully within it.

RESISTANCE
LVL 4
QUEST IV

Building Your Legend

What story will my life tell?

Knights went on quests to build their legends. What story will they tell of you? Take on hard things and learn new things. The kind of life that becomes a legend.
